# History · Ages 15–16 · The Cold War and after
Q: Put these Cold War events in order. Start with the earliest.
Order: Berlin Airlift, Korean War begins, Berlin Wall built, Cuban Missile Crisis
Why: They began in 1948, 1950, 1961 and 1962.

Q: The Cuban Missile Crisis of 1962 was a stand-off between the United States and which country?
A) the Soviet Union*
B) China
C) France
Why: The Soviet Union had placed nuclear missiles on Cuba, close to the United States.

Q: In which year was NATO founded?
Slider: 1940 to 1960 = 1949 ±0
Why: Twelve countries signed the North Atlantic Treaty in April 1949.

Q: In which year did the Soviet Union come to an end?
Slider: 1980 to 2000 = 1991 ±0
Why: The Soviet Union was dissolved in December 1991 and split into fifteen countries.

Q: In which year was the Warsaw Pact formed?
Slider: 1945 to 1965 = 1955 ±0
Why: The Soviet Union and its allies signed the pact in May 1955, six years after NATO was founded.

Q: Match each leader to the country they led.
Match: Mikhail Gorbachev = Soviet Union; Mao Zedong = China; John F. Kennedy = United States; Charles de Gaulle = France; Nelson Mandela = South Africa
Why: Gorbachev led the Soviet Union from 1985 to 1991, Mao led China from 1949, Kennedy became US president in 1961, de Gaulle French president in 1959 and Mandela South Africa's president in 1994.

Q: Sort these events into those before and those after the Second World War ended in 1945.
Sort: Before 1945 = Wall Street Crash, Treaty of Versailles, Battle of Britain; After 1945 = NHS founded, Suez Crisis, Berlin Wall falls
Why: The Wall Street Crash was 1929, Versailles 1919 and the Battle of Britain 1940. The NHS began in 1948, Suez was 1956 and the Wall fell in 1989.

Q: What did the Marshall Plan provide?
A) American money to rebuild Western Europe*
B) a military alliance between European states
C) a free trade area for Asia
Why: The United States offered aid from 1948 to help countries recover after the war.

Q: Which organisation was set up in 1945 to help keep world peace?
A) the United Nations*
B) the League of Nations
C) NATO
Why: The League of Nations began in 1920 and failed to stop the Second World War. The United Nations replaced it.

Q: Why did East Germany build the Berlin Wall in 1961?
A) to stop East Germans fleeing to the West*
B) to stop trade between East and West Germany
C) to protect Berlin from floods
Why: Millions of East Germans had left for the West. The wall stopped them, and the border opened in November 1989.

# Geography · Ages 15–16 · Plate tectonics, rivers and climate
Q: Put the parts of a river in order, from its start to the sea.
Order: source, upper course, middle course, lower course, mouth
Why: A river begins at its source in the hills and ends at its mouth, where it meets the sea.

Q: Sort the places by the type of plate boundary they lie on.
Sort: Constructive = Mid-Atlantic Ridge, Iceland; Destructive = Andes, Mariana Trench; Conservative = San Andreas Fault
Why: Plates move apart at constructive boundaries, collide at destructive ones and slide past each other at conservative ones.

Q: Match each river feature to what it is.
Match: Meander = a bend in a river; Delta = deposits at the mouth; Waterfall = a sudden drop in a river; Oxbow lake = a lake left by a loop
Why: A meander is a bend. If a river cuts through the neck of a meander, the old loop becomes an oxbow lake. A delta forms where a river drops its load at the sea.

Q: What most often causes a tsunami?
A) an earthquake under the sea*
B) a strong wind
C) a very high tide
Why: A sudden movement of the sea floor pushes the water above it.

Q: About what percentage of the Earth's surface is covered by oceans?
Slider: 0 to 100 = 71 ±1 %
Why: Oceans cover about 71 per cent of the surface.

Q: Which supercontinent began to break apart about 200 million years ago?
A) Pangaea*
B) Rodinia
C) Eurasia
Why: Pangaea began to split about 200 million years ago. Rodinia formed over a billion years earlier.

Q: Rain that falls when moist air is forced up over hills is called...
A) relief rainfall*
B) convectional rainfall
C) frontal rainfall
Why: Hills force moist air upwards. It cools as it rises, so water vapour condenses into cloud and rain.

Q: Which gas has added most to the enhanced greenhouse effect?
A) carbon dioxide*
B) methane
C) nitrogen
Why: Burning fossil fuels has raised carbon dioxide the most. Methane traps more heat per molecule, but there is far less of it.

Q: Choose the word that completes the coastal sentence.
Gap: Waves ___ the base of a cliff, making a wave-cut notch. = erode | extra: deposit, protect
Why: Hydraulic action and abrasion wear away the base of the cliff. The notch grows until the cliff above collapses.

# Bible & Faith · Ages 15–16 · Acts and the letters
Q: Put these events in Acts in order, as the book tells them.
Order: Pentecost, Seven helpers chosen, Saul meets Jesus, Paul's first journey
Why: These are told in Acts chapters 2, 6, 9 and 13.

Q: Which gospel writer is traditionally named as the author of the book of Acts?
A) Luke*
B) Mark
C) John
Why: Acts opens by addressing the same person as Luke's gospel, so the two are read as a pair.

Q: Match each New Testament book to the kind of writing it is.
Match: Acts = a history of the church; Romans = a letter from Paul; Revelation = visions and prophecy; Luke = an account of Jesus
Why: The New Testament holds gospels, a history of the early church in Acts, letters, and the visions of Revelation.

Q: Sort the people by whether they appear in the Old Testament or the New Testament.
Sort: Old Testament = Moses, David, Ruth; New Testament = Peter, Paul, Lydia
Why: Moses, David and Ruth appear in the Old Testament. Peter appears in the gospels and Acts, and Paul and Lydia appear in Acts.

Q: On which road, according to Acts, did Saul meet Jesus in a bright light?
A) the road to Damascus*
B) the road to Jericho
C) the road to Emmaus
Why: Acts 9 tells how Saul, on the way to Damascus, became a follower of Jesus.

Q: In which city were the followers of Jesus first called Christians, according to Acts?
A) Antioch*
B) Jerusalem
C) Rome
Why: Acts 11 says they were first called Christians in Antioch.

Q: How many books are in the New Testament?
Slider: 0 to 40 = 27 ±0
Why: There are four gospels, Acts, 21 letters and Revelation.

Q: What does the word "gospel" mean?
A) good news*
B) holy law
C) sacred song
Why: The word comes from an old English word meaning good news.

Q: Which follower of Jesus is stoned to death in Acts 7?
A) Stephen*
B) Peter
C) Barnabas
Why: Acts 7 tells how Stephen was stoned.
