# English · Ages 4–5 · Rhymes and sentences
Q: Which word rhymes with "hat"?
A) mat*
B) cup
C) dog
Why: "hat" and "mat" end with the same sound.

Q: Which word rhymes with "bun"?
A) sit
B) run*
C) hop
Why: "bun" and "run" end with the same sound.

Q: Which word rhymes with "pen"?
A) pig
B) bus
C) hen*
Why: "pen" and "hen" end with the same sound.

Q: Which word rhymes with "log"?
A) leg
B) cat
C) dog*
Why: "log" and "dog" end with the same sound.

Q: Which word rhymes with "star"?
A) sun
B) car*
C) tree
Why: "star" and "car" end with the same sound.

Q: Finish the sentence about the cat.
Gap: The cat sat on the ___. = mat | extra: ran, jump
Why: "Mat" rhymes with "cat" and makes sense.

Q: Finish the sentence about lunch.
Gap: I eat my lunch with a ___. = spoon | extra: shoe, hat
Why: You eat with a spoon.

Q: Finish the sentence about bedtime.
Gap: We go to bed at ___. = night | extra: lunch, break
Why: Bedtime is at night.

Q: Build a sentence about a dog.
Order: The, dog, ran
Why: A sentence starts with a capital letter: "The dog ran."

Q: Build a sentence about apples.
Order: I, like, red, apples
Why: "I like red apples" says who, what they do and what about.
