# History · Ages 9–11 · A timeline of Britain
Q: Put these groups in order of when they arrived or ruled in Britain. Start with the earliest.
Order: Romans, Vikings, Normans, Tudors, Victorians
Why: Romans came in AD 43, Vikings from AD 793, Normans in 1066, the Tudors from 1485 and Victoria became queen in 1837.

Q: In which year did the Normans win the Battle of Hastings?
Slider: 1000 to 1100 = 1066 ±0
Why: William of Normandy beat King Harold in October 1066.

Q: In which year did the Spanish Armada sail against England?
Slider: 1500 to 1600 = 1588 ±0
Why: The Armada sailed in 1588, in the reign of Elizabeth I.

Q: Sort the people into the age they lived in.
Sort: Tudor = Henry VIII, Elizabeth I, Anne Boleyn; Victorian = Queen Victoria, Charles Dickens, Prince Albert
Why: The Tudors ruled from 1485 to 1603. Victoria was queen from 1837 to 1901.

Q: Which king won the throne in 1485 and began the Tudor family of rulers?
A) Henry VII*
B) Henry VIII
C) Edward VI
Why: Henry VII won the throne in 1485 and his son was Henry VIII.

Q: Which Roman wall crosses the north of England?
A) Hadrian's Wall*
B) the Great Wall
C) Offa's Dyke
Why: Hadrian's Wall was built in about AD 122.

Q: The Vikings came from Scandinavia.
A) True*
B) False
Why: The Vikings sailed from lands that are now Denmark, Norway and Sweden.

Q: Match each person from Britain's past to what they are known for.
Match: Boudicca = revolted against Rome; Alfred the Great = fought the Vikings; William the Conqueror = won at Hastings; Elizabeth I = Tudor queen
Why: Boudicca led a revolt against the Romans, Alfred defended Wessex against the Vikings, William won at Hastings in 1066 and Elizabeth I ruled in Tudor times.

Q: Which of these happened first?
A) the Roman invasion of Britain*
B) the Battle of Hastings
C) the Spanish Armada
Why: The Romans invaded in AD 43, the Battle of Hastings was in 1066 and the Armada sailed in 1588.

# Geography · Ages 9–11 · Europe: countries and capitals
Q: Match these countries to their capital cities: Poland, Portugal, Austria, Greece and Ireland.
Match: Poland = Warsaw; Portugal = Lisbon; Austria = Vienna; Greece = Athens; Ireland = Dublin
Why: A capital is the city where a country's government sits.

Q: Match these countries to their capital cities: Germany, Norway, Sweden, Hungary and Belgium.
Match: Germany = Berlin; Norway = Oslo; Sweden = Stockholm; Hungary = Budapest; Belgium = Brussels
Why: Each capital is the city where that country's government sits.

Q: Sort the countries into islands and mainland.
Sort: Island = Cyprus, Iceland, Malta; Mainland = France, Poland, Austria
Why: An island country is surrounded by sea. The mainland countries share land borders with their neighbours.

Q: Which is the longest river in Europe?
A) the Volga*
B) the Thames
C) the Rhine
Why: The Volga is in Russia and is about 3,500 kilometres long.

Q: Which mountains lie between France and Spain?
A) the Pyrenees*
B) the Alps
C) the Andes
Why: The Pyrenees form a natural border between France and Spain.

Q: Which sea lies to the south of Europe?
A) the Mediterranean Sea*
B) the Baltic Sea
C) the North Sea
Why: The Mediterranean lies between Europe and Africa.

Q: How many countries are in the United Kingdom?
Slider: 0 to 10 = 4 ±0
Why: England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland make four.

Q: Which country is shaped like a boot?
A) Italy*
B) Spain
C) Norway
Why: Italy has a long, boot-shaped coast.

Q: Which city is the capital of Northern Ireland?
A) Belfast*
B) Derry
C) Lisburn
Why: Belfast is the capital and the largest city of Northern Ireland.

# Bible & Faith · Ages 9–11 · Books of the Bible
Q: Sort the books into the Old Testament and the New Testament.
Sort: Old Testament = Genesis, Psalms, Isaiah; New Testament = Matthew, Acts, Romans
Why: The Old Testament was written before Jesus was born. The New Testament is about Jesus and the first Christians.

Q: Put the first four books of the New Testament in order.
Order: Matthew, Mark, Luke, John
Why: Matthew, Mark, Luke and John are the four gospels, and they come first in the New Testament in that order.

Q: Put the first five books of the Bible in order.
Order: Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy
Why: These five books come first in the Bible.

Q: How many books are in most Protestant Bibles?
Slider: 0 to 100 = 66 ±0
Why: There are 39 books in the Old Testament and 27 in the New Testament.

Q: What is the last book of the Bible?
A) Revelation*
B) Genesis
C) Acts
Why: Revelation is the final book of the New Testament, and so of the whole Bible.

Q: Which book begins with the story of creation?
A) Genesis*
B) Exodus
C) Psalms
Why: Genesis means "beginning".

Q: Match each book to what it contains.
Match: Psalms = songs and prayers; Acts = the first Christians; Genesis = creation; Proverbs = wise sayings
Why: The Bible is a library of many kinds of writing.

Q: Paul wrote many of the letters in the New Testament.
A) True*
B) False
Why: Letters to churches, such as Romans and Galatians, are by Paul.

Q: Which gospel begins with the words "In the beginning was the Word"?
A) John*
B) Mark
C) Matthew
Why: John begins with these words.
