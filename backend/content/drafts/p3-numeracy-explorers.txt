# Maths · Ages 6–8 · Adding and subtracting to 100
Q: What is 47 + 30?
Slider: 0 to 100 = 77 ±0
Why: Add the tens: 40 + 30 = 70, then add the 7 to make 77.

Q: What is 63 − 20?
Slider: 0 to 100 = 43 ±0
Why: Take away 2 tens from 6 tens to make 4 tens, so 43.

Q: What is 38 + 25?
Slider: 0 to 100 = 63 ±0
Why: 38 + 20 = 58, then 58 + 5 = 63.

Q: What is 91 − 47?
Slider: 0 to 100 = 44 ±0
Why: 91 − 40 = 51, then 51 − 7 = 44.

Q: What is 56 + 28?
Slider: 0 to 100 = 84 ±0
Why: 56 + 20 = 76, then 76 + 8 = 84.

Q: What is 100 − 35?
Slider: 0 to 100 = 65 ±0
Why: 100 − 30 = 70, then 70 − 5 = 65.

Q: What number is 10 more than 76?
Slider: 0 to 100 = 86 ±0
Why: Adding 10 adds one to the tens digit: 86.

Q: What number is 10 less than 52?
Slider: 0 to 100 = 42 ±0
Why: Taking away 10 takes one from the tens digit: 42.

Q: What is 29 + 31?
Slider: 0 to 100 = 60 ±0
Why: 29 + 1 = 30, then 30 + 30 = 60.

Q: What is 84 − 39?
Slider: 0 to 100 = 45 ±0
Why: 84 − 40 = 44, then add 1 back to make 45.

# Maths · Ages 6–8 · Patterns, odd and even, and order
Q: What comes next? 2, 4, 6, 8, …
Slider: 0 to 20 = 10 ±0
Why: The numbers go up by 2 each time.

Q: What comes next? 5, 10, 15, 20, …
Slider: 0 to 50 = 25 ±0
Why: The numbers go up by 5 each time.

Q: What comes next? 30, 27, 24, 21, …
Slider: 0 to 50 = 18 ±0
Why: The numbers go down by 3 each time.

Q: What comes next? 1, 3, 5, 7, …
Slider: 0 to 20 = 9 ±0
Why: The odd numbers go up by 2 each time.

Q: Sort the numbers into odd and even.
Sort: Odd = 3, 15, 7; Even = 8, 12, 20
Why: Even numbers end in 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8. Odd numbers end in 1, 3, 5, 7 or 9.

Q: Sort the numbers into those you say in the 5 times table and those you do not.
Sort: In the 5 times table = 10, 25, 40; Not in it = 12, 33, 47
Why: Numbers in the 5 times table end in 0 or 5.

Q: Put these numbers in order, smallest first: 45, 18, 72, 9.
Order: 9, 18, 45, 72
Why: Compare the tens first: 9 is under 10, then 18, 45 and 72.

Q: Put these numbers in order, biggest first: 56, 65, 38, 83.
Order: 83, 65, 56, 38
Why: Look at the tens first: 8, 6, 5 and 3.

Q: Match each number to its double.
Match: 3 = 6; 5 = 10; 8 = 16; 6 = 12
Why: To double a number, add it to itself.
