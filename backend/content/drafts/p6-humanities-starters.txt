# Bible & Faith · Ages 4–5 · The Christmas story
Q: Who were the parents of baby Jesus in the Bible story?
A) Mary and Joseph*
B) Adam and Eve
C) Noah and his wife
Why: Luke's gospel names Mary and Joseph as the parents of Jesus.

Q: Where did Mary lay the baby Jesus, the Bible says?
A) in a manger*
B) in a boat
C) in a tent
Why: A manger is a box that holds hay for animals. Luke 2 tells this part of the story.

Q: In Luke's story, who told the shepherds the good news?
A) angels*
B) kings
C) sailors
Why: Luke says that angels told the shepherds about the baby.

Q: In Matthew's story, what did the wise men follow?
A) a star*
B) a river
C) a road sign
Why: Matthew says that wise men saw a star and followed it.

Q: What did the wise men bring for Jesus?
A) gifts*
B) fish
C) toys
Why: Matthew says that they brought gifts of gold, frankincense and myrrh.

Q: Put the Christmas story in order, as Luke tells it.
Order: Mary and Joseph travel, Jesus is born, shepherds visit
Why: In Luke, Mary and Joseph go to Bethlehem, Jesus is born and then the shepherds come.

Q: Christians celebrate the birth of Jesus at Christmas.
A) True*
B) False
Why: Christmas is the Christian celebration of the birth of Jesus.

Q: Match each person to their part in the story.
Match: Mary = mother of Jesus; Shepherd = looked after sheep; Angel = brought good news; Wise men = brought gifts
Why: These are some of the people in the Christmas story in the Bible.

# Bible & Faith · Ages 4–5 · Easter and other celebrations
Q: What do Christians celebrate at Easter?
A) Jesus coming back to life*
B) the first day of summer
C) a harvest
Why: The gospels say that Jesus came back to life on the third day, a Sunday.

Q: What do many people give or eat at Easter?
A) Easter eggs*
B) Christmas trees
C) fireworks
Why: Easter eggs are a favourite Easter treat.

Q: When do Christians celebrate the birth of Jesus?
A) Christmas*
B) Easter
C) Harvest
Why: Christmas is the celebration of the birth of Jesus.

Q: Which of these is the Christian holy book?
A) the Bible*
B) a recipe book
C) a map
Why: The Bible is the holy book of Christians.

Q: What do we call the building where many Christians meet to pray and sing?
A) a church*
B) a shop
C) a school
Why: People meet in a church to pray, sing and listen to Bible stories.

Q: Match each celebration to something people do.
Match: Christmas = decorate a tree; Easter = eat chocolate eggs; Harvest = share food
Why: Each celebration has its own favourite things to do.

Q: Sort the things into Christmas and Easter.
Sort: Christmas = tree, stocking, cracker; Easter = egg, hot cross bun, bunny
Why: These are things people often see at Christmas and at Easter.

Q: Hot cross buns are a treat at Easter.
A) True*
B) False
Why: Many people eat hot cross buns at Easter.

Q: In John's gospel, the crowds waved what as Jesus rode into the city?
A) palm branches*
B) flags
C) umbrellas
Why: John's gospel says that the crowds waved branches of palm trees.
