# Science · Ages 9–11 · Solids, liquids and gases
Q: Sort the things into solid, liquid and gas.
Sort: Solid = stone, wood; Liquid = water, milk; Gas = air, oxygen
Why: A solid keeps its shape, a liquid pours and a gas spreads out to fill its space.

Q: What is it called when a liquid turns into a gas?
A) evaporation*
B) freezing
C) melting
Why: Evaporation happens when a liquid warms up and its particles escape into the air.

Q: At what temperature does pure water boil at sea level, in degrees Celsius?
Slider: 0 to 150 = 100 ±0 °C
Why: Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius and freezes at 0 degrees Celsius.

Q: Put the water cycle in order. Start with the water in the sea.
Order: water evaporates, vapour condenses, rain falls, water collects
Why: The Sun warms the water, the vapour cools into clouds, rain falls and the water collects again.

Q: A gas fills the space it is in.
A) True*
B) False
Why: The particles of a gas spread out in every direction.

Q: A solid changes shape when you put it in a different container.
A) True
B) False*
Why: A solid keeps its own shape. A liquid takes the shape of its container.

Q: What is melting?
A) a solid turning into a liquid*
B) a liquid turning into a solid
C) a gas turning into a liquid
Why: Ice melts into water when it warms up.

Q: Match each change to its name.
Match: Solid to liquid = melting; Liquid to solid = freezing; Liquid to gas = evaporating; Gas to liquid = condensing
Why: These four changes happen when something is heated or cooled.

Q: Which state of matter has a fixed shape?
A) solid*
B) liquid
C) gas
Why: The particles of a solid are held tightly in place.

# Science · Ages 9–11 · Light, sound and electricity
Q: A material that lets electricity pass through is called a...
A) conductor*
B) insulator
C) shadow
Why: Metals are conductors. Rubber and plastic are insulators.

Q: Sort the things into conductors and insulators.
Sort: Conductors = copper wire, steel spoon, foil; Insulators = rubber glove, plastic ruler, wooden spoon
Why: Metals let electricity through. Rubber, plastic and wood do not.

Q: Light travels in straight lines.
A) True*
B) False
Why: This is why a shadow has a sharp edge.

Q: What makes a shadow?
A) something that blocks the light*
B) a loud noise
C) a magnet
Why: A shadow forms behind an object that light cannot pass through.

Q: Sound is made when something...
A) vibrates*
B) glows
C) freezes
Why: A vibration is a tiny, quick movement back and forth.

Q: Sound can travel through space.
A) True
B) False*
Why: Sound needs something to travel through, such as air. Space has almost none.

Q: We see lightning before we hear thunder because light is faster than sound.
A) True*
B) False
Why: Light travels much faster than sound, so it reaches us first.

Q: Match each part of a simple circuit to its job.
Match: Cell = gives the power; Wire = carries electricity; Bulb = gives out light; Switch = turns it on and off
Why: A circuit is a complete loop. The switch opens or closes the loop.

Q: Which of these gives out its own light?
A) the Sun*
B) the Moon
C) a mirror
Why: The Sun makes light. The Moon and a mirror only reflect it.

Q: A fast vibration makes a sound with a...
A) high pitch*
B) low pitch
C) no pitch
Why: The faster the vibration, the higher the sound.

# Science · Ages 9–11 · Rocks, the Earth and space
Q: Put the planets in order from the Sun. Start with the closest.
Order: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars
Why: These four small, rocky planets are the closest to the Sun.

Q: Put the planets in order from the Sun. Start with Jupiter.
Order: Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune
Why: These are the four outer planets. Neptune is the farthest.

Q: Sort the rocks by how they formed.
Sort: Sedimentary = sandstone, limestone; Igneous = granite, basalt; Metamorphic = marble, slate
Why: Sedimentary rocks form from layers, igneous rocks from cooled magma or lava and metamorphic rocks from heat and pressure.

Q: What causes day and night?
A) The Earth spinning*
B) The Moon moving in front of the Sun
C) The Sun going out
Why: The Earth turns once a day, so each place faces the Sun and then faces away.

Q: How many planets are in our solar system?
Slider: 0 to 12 = 8 ±0
Why: There are eight planets, from Mercury to Neptune.

Q: Which planet is known as the Red Planet?
A) Mars*
B) Venus
C) Saturn
Why: Mars looks red because of the rusty dust on its ground.

Q: The Sun is a star at the centre of our solar system.
A) True*
B) False
Why: The Sun is a star: a huge ball of hot gas that gives out light and heat.

Q: Put the layers of the Earth in order, from the outside to the centre.
Order: crust, mantle, outer core, inner core
Why: We live on the thin crust. The inner core is the hottest part.

Q: What do we call the remains of a living thing that are found in rock?
A) a fossil*
B) a crystal
C) a mineral
Why: Fossils are the remains or marks of plants and animals that lived long ago.

Q: How long does the Earth take to travel once around the Sun?
A) one year*
B) one day
C) one month
Why: The Earth's trip around the Sun takes about 365 days.
