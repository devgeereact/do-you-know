# Science · Ages 12–14 · Atoms, elements and reactions
Q: Match each chemical symbol to its element.
Match: Na = sodium; Fe = iron; K = potassium; Au = gold; Cu = copper
Why: Many symbols come from Latin-based names: natrium, ferrum, kalium, aurum and cuprum.

Q: Sort the substances into elements and compounds.
Sort: Elements = oxygen, iron, gold; Compounds = water, carbon dioxide, salt
Why: An element has one kind of atom. A compound has two or more kinds joined together.

Q: What is the pH of a neutral solution such as pure water?
Slider: 0 to 14 = 7 ±0
Why: Below 7 is acid and above 7 is alkali.

Q: Which gas is made when a reactive metal such as magnesium reacts with an acid?
A) hydrogen*
B) oxygen
C) helium
Why: The metal and the acid make a salt and hydrogen gas.

Q: Sort the liquids into acid, alkali and neutral.
Sort: Acid = lemon juice, vinegar; Alkali = bleach, oven cleaner; Neutral = pure water
Why: Acids have a pH below 7, alkalis above 7 and neutral substances are at 7.

Q: Which particles in an atom have a negative charge?
A) electrons*
B) protons
C) neutrons
Why: Electrons are negative, protons are positive and neutrons have no charge.

Q: What is the name of the reaction when a fuel burns in oxygen, giving out heat and light?
A) combustion*
B) neutralisation
C) condensation
Why: Combustion is a fuel reacting with oxygen. It makes new substances such as carbon dioxide and water.

Q: In a chemical reaction, new substances are made.
A) True*
B) False
Why: The atoms are rearranged to make different substances.

Q: Melting ice is a chemical reaction.
A) True
B) False*
Why: Melting is a physical change. No new substance is made.

Q: Put these in order, from the particles with the least energy to the most.
Order: solid, liquid, gas
Why: The particles of a gas move fastest and spread the furthest.

Q: Choose the missing word.
Gap: In a reaction, the total ___ stays the same. = mass | extra: volume, colour
Why: Atoms are not lost or made in a reaction, so the total mass stays the same.
